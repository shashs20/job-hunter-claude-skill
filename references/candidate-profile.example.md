## Important Accuracy Rules

Do not claim the candidate has professional experience with a technology, responsibility or business function when the evidence comes only from education or a personal project.

Clearly distinguish:

- Professional experience
- Academic experience
- Personal/project experience

Never invent experience to improve the candidate's apparent fit.

Transferable experience may be identified, but it must be described accurately.
