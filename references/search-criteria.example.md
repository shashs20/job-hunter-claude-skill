# Job Search Criteria — Example

This is an example search configuration for the Job Hunter Skill.

Replace these criteria with your own requirements.

## Main Objective

Find vacancies where the candidate has a realistic chance of securing an interview while gaining useful experience for a future career in Business Analysis or Business Operations.

## Priority Roles

Search primarily for:

1. Operations Coordinator
2. Operations Assistant
3. Business Operations Assistant
4. Project Coordinator
5. Project Assistant
6. Project Support Officer
7. Programme Coordinator
8. Programme Assistant
9. Implementation Coordinator
10. Onboarding Coordinator
11. Data / Reporting Assistant
12. Junior Business Analyst

Also consider closely related titles when the responsibilities are a strong match.

## Career Value

Prefer vacancies involving:

- Business processes
- Operations
- Projects
- Systems
- Data
- Reporting
- Excel
- Stakeholder coordination
- Documentation
- Process improvement
- Implementation
- Change
- KPIs
- Cross-functional working

## Seniority

Prioritise:

- Entry level
- Junior
- Assistant
- Coordinator
- Graduate
- Approximately 0–2 years of required experience

Avoid roles that clearly require substantial professional experience.

## Location

Example preferences:

- London
- Greater London
- Hybrid roles
- Remote UK roles

## Exclusions

Do not recommend:

- Apprenticeships
- Senior management roles
- Software development roles
- Cold-calling roles
- Sales-heavy roles
- Call-centre jobs
- Personal Assistant roles
- Executive Assistant roles
- Jobs primarily involving diary management

## Application Strategy

Prioritise realistic applications over speculative applications.

Most recommendations should be roles where the candidate has a credible interview chance.

A small number of stretch opportunities may be included when:

- The candidate meets most essential requirements
- Missing requirements are desirable rather than mandatory
- The role provides particularly strong career value

## Vacancy Quality

Prefer:

- Permanent roles
- Full-time roles
- Clear job descriptions
- Reputable employers
- Roles with genuine development potential

## Live Vacancy Requirement

Only recommend jobs that appear to be currently accepting applications.

Where possible:

1. Open the vacancy.
2. Check the application deadline.
3. Check whether an application route still exists.
4. Prefer official employer career pages.
5. Avoid expired, closed or removed vacancies.

## Search Quality

Do not simply return the first search results.

Search multiple relevant job titles and sources.

Prioritise quality over quantity.

A smaller list of genuinely suitable vacancies is better than a large list of weak matches.
